# Roadmap

Energy Prices Manager will continue to broaden the tariff types it can manage
for Home Assistant's Energy Dashboard.

## Planned

- **Returned electricity pricing** — maintain separate prices for electricity
  supplied back to the grid.
- **Dynamic energy contracts** — support day-ahead electricity prices at hourly
  and quarter-hourly intervals, so users with dynamic contracts can use the
  app as well.

The roadmap describes intended improvements, not committed delivery dates.
